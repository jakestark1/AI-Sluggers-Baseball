How to play

Play three innings against the CPU, with extra innings for ties. Every at-bat starts with one ball and one strike: two more strikes make an out; three more balls earn a walk. Three outs switch sides.

Your away team bats in the top half; your home team bats in the bottom half. To pitch: aim with your pointer or arrow keys, choose a pitch with 1–3, then throw when the accuracy marker is centered. To bat: press Space, tap the field, or use Swing when the timing ring turns green. Hits advance runners automatically. Good plays charge your super meter; E arms a ghost pitch or fire bat before the next pitch. P pauses. Sound toggles the arcade sound effects.
